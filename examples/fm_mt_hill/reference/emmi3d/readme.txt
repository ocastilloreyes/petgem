Here are the data from the trapezoidal hill model, taken on a line parallel to the x-axis, passing through the center of the hill.

The file 'Coordinates.dat' has the (x,y,z) coordinates of each of the 41 stations, listed in order of the x coordinate. The other files are apparent resistivity (Rxy, Ryx) in ohm-m and phase angles (Pxy, Pyx) in degrees.

We've tried to follow exactly the survey line shown in Figure 2 of your paper. The data files are organized by frequency, which is listed in the first column. The first line has the results at 2 Hz, and the remaining ones are at the listed frequencies.
